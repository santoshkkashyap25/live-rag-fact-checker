# FactGuard AI - Chrome Extension

> Highlight any statement on Twitter, Reddit, or news sites $\rightarrow$ Right-click **"Verify with FactGuard"** $\rightarrow$ Instant verdict and citations.

---

## 🚀 How to Install in Chrome, Brave, or Edge

1. Open your browser and navigate to:
   - **Chrome**: `chrome://extensions`
   - **Brave**: `brave://extensions`
   - **Edge**: `edge://extensions`
2. Enable **Developer mode** (toggle in the top-right corner).
3. Click the **"Load unpacked"** button in the top-left.
4. Select the `extension/` folder in this repository.
5. The **FactGuard AI** shield icon will appear in your browser toolbar! Pin it for quick access.

---

## 🛡️ How to Use

1. **Highlight text anywhere**:
   - Highlight any claim or controversial post on Twitter/X, Reddit, or a news article.
   - Right-click and choose **"Verify with FactGuard: \"...\""**.
2. **Instant Popup**:
   - The FactGuard extension popup appears with your highlighted claim pre-filled.
   - Click **Verify Claim** to get an instant verdict (`TRUE`, `FALSE`, or `UNVERIFIABLE`), confidence percentage, reasoning, and clickable citations.
3. **Switch Servers**:
   - Use the dropdown to choose between **Render Cloud (Live)** and **Localhost (Port 8000)** when running FactGuard locally with Ollama.
